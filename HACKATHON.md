# SafeNest hackathon notes

## The problem

Families in Kenya often discover harm after a child is already distressed. Generic parental-control apps ask for passwords, watch silently, or dump people into a chatbot that invents helpline numbers.

## The product

SafeNest is a **safety assistant**, not a spy tool and not a court.

Something happened online → screenshot or description → structured AI analysis → risk type + severity + why + what to do → evidence locker → verified Kenyan resources.

## Why this is Kenyan, not Silicon Valley

- Mobile-first, low-bandwidth uploads (4MB cap, compressed by the phone)
- English now, Kiswahili toggle on the welcome and home screens
- Resources are curated: 116, 1195, 999/112, KE-CIRT, Fichua kwa DCI
- The model is forbidden from inventing contacts
- Works without an OpenAI key so a demo never dies on Wi-Fi

## What AI does / does not do

Does: flag indicators, explain in plain language, suggest proportionate next steps.

Does not: declare guilt, demand passwords, keylog, or replace a counsellor, police officer, or parent.
